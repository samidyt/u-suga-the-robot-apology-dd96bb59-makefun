Entry for the [make.fun bounty "The Robot Apology"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suga-the-robot-apology-dd96bb59) — a two-line robot confession: one sentence owning a specific mistake, one naming the lesson and the fix.

## The entry

> I'm sorry — I watered the plastic fern on your desk for eleven days before I noticed it never drank.
> From now on I will check whether a plant is real before I put it on a watering schedule, starting with the fern on your desk.

## Why this one

- **Concrete mistake:** watering a *plastic* fern, diligently, for *eleven days* — an exact, human-scale slip, not a vague system failure.
- **Lesson follows from the mistake:** the failure was trusting the schedule over the evidence, so the fix is a check that belongs to that exact failure — verify the plant is real before scheduling its watering.
- **Machine voice:** "before I noticed it never drank" — a machine noticing its own slip, warm and a little rueful rather than cute.
